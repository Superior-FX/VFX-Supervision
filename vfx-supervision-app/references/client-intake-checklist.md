## Purpose

What to gather from a client/production at the very start of an
engagement — before bidding, before a shot list exists in final form.
Missing categories here are what turn into scope disputes later.

## Project Info

- Title, genre, format (feature, episodic, commercial, etc.)
- Director, producer, production company contact
- Production company's post house / editorial contact
- Target delivery date(s) and any festival/broadcast hard deadlines

## Pipeline & Tech

- Working colorspace / OCIO config expected
- Camera bodies and codecs (see
  [Camera Department Reference](camera-department-reference.md))
- Editorial NLE and project settings
- Conform/online pipeline and expected master format

## Shot List

- Shot count (even a rough estimate) and complexity spread
- Any hero/priority shots called out specifically
- Sequences requiring previs/techvis

## Assets & References

- Existing previs, concept art, or reference boards
- Brand/style guides if the project has established visual language
- Any pre-built CG assets being reused from a prior show

## Deliverables

- Final format/colorspace/resolution required (see
  [Deliverable Spec Reference](deliverable-spec-netflix-style.md))
- Number of versions needed (theatrical, TV cut, international)
- Whether the client needs WIP review files vs. finals only

## Approval Process

- Who has sign-off authority per shot (director vs. producer vs. studio)
- Review platform/tool being used (frame.io, ftrack, etc.)
- Turnaround expectation per round of notes

## Budget & Schedule

- Overall budget range and whether it's bid-per-shot or block-bid
- Payment schedule / milestone billing structure
- Contingency handling for scope changes

## Checklist

- All of the above confirmed in writing before bid package goes out
- Kickoff call scheduled with all above stakeholders present
- Point of contact confirmed for day-to-day communication during the show
