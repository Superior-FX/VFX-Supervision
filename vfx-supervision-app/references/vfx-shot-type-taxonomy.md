## Purpose

A checklist of VFX categories to run against every scene during script
breakdown. Action lines that read as "practical" on the page often carry
a VFX enhancement in practice — this taxonomy exists to catch those before
bidding, not after dailies.

## Environment

- Set extension
- Sky replacement
- Digital matte painting (DMP)
- Full digital environment build
- Weather/atmospherics (rain, snow, fog, dust storms)
- Day-for-night conversion
- Set dressing removal/addition
- Time-of-day or seasonal change

## Characters

- CG creature
- Digital double
- De-aging / age-up
- Face replacement
- Crowd replication / tiling
- Wire and rig removal (on-camera performer)
- Prosthetic/makeup enhancement
- Body augmentation (injuries, transformations)

## Objects & Effects

- Fire, smoke, explosion simulation
- Water simulation
- Destruction / debris simulation
- Vehicle CG or vehicle replacement
- Muzzle flash / gunfire FX
- Screen replacement / comp (monitors, phones, windows)
- Practical FX enhancement (augmenting a real gag)
- Particle FX (dust, sparks, magic/energy effects)

## Post / Invisible

- Color/beauty cleanup
- Logo / product removal (clearance-driven)
- Continuity fix
- Stabilization
- Split screen
- Time remap / speed ramp
- Invisible wire/rig removal (safety harness, stunt rigging)
- Crew/equipment removal (boom mics, shadows, reflections)

## Script Breakdown Tip

When flagging a scene, don't stop at the obvious VFX beat (the creature,
the explosion). Re-read the scene against the Post/Invisible list
specifically — clearance removals, rig removals, and continuity fixes are
the shots that get missed at bid time and show up as unbudgeted change
orders in post.
