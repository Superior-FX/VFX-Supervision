# Getting set up on the Sup-FX App (GOTP)

Hey Jon, here's how to get set up on the Sup-FX App for GOTP. Start step 1 first: the download can run while you do the rest.

## 1. Get the GOTP folder onto your computer

1. Open Google Drive in your browser and go to **Shared with me**.
2. Right-click the **GOTP** folder Will shared and choose **Organize → Add shortcut to My Drive**.
3. Install **Google Drive for desktop** from <https://www.google.com/drive/download/> and sign in with the same Google account.
4. In File Explorer, open the new Google Drive letter and go to **My Drive**.
5. Right-click **GOTP** and choose **Offline access → Available offline**. It downloads in the background; let it run.

> Don't download the folder as a zip from the browser. That copy isn't connected to Drive, so nothing would sync between us.

## 2. Create your account

1. Open **<https://app.superior-fx.com>** in **Chrome or Edge**. Other browsers can't connect to folders.
2. Click **New here? Create an account** and sign up with **the same email Will invited**.
3. Click the confirmation link in the email, then sign in.

## 3. Connect the folder

1. Open **GOTP**, then **Post Reports**.
2. Click **Reconnect Folder…** on the banner and pick the **GOTP** folder on your Google Drive letter.
3. Click **Allow** when Chrome asks.

After a Chrome restart you may see **Allow Access** instead. Just click it.

## 4. Add the link to the Superior-FX site (Base44)

Paste this into the Base44 AI chat for the superior-fx.com site:

> Add an admin-only link to the site's navigation labeled **"Sup-FX App"** that opens **https://app.superior-fx.com** in a **new browser tab** (target="_blank", rel="noopener noreferrer").
> - Show it **only** when the visitor is signed in **and** their user role is **admin**. Hide it completely, including from the HTML and the mobile menu, for logged-out visitors and for signed-in users who aren't admins.
> - It's a plain external link. Do **not** embed the app in an iframe or a page on this site.
> - Don't change anything else about the site, its pages, or its public navigation.

Then make sure Will and you are **admin** users on the Base44 site so the link shows up.

## Good to know

- Shot, task and note changes show up for both of us live.
- Videos and plates travel through Google Drive, so a new upload can take a minute to reach you. If the app says **"Still syncing from Google Drive"**, just wait; it retries on its own.

Let Will know once you're connected and we'll test it together.
